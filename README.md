# airbnb-tableau-analysis
Interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing Airbnb listings data to explore pricing trends, listing types, availability, and neighborhood insights. Visualizes key metrics to help understand the Airbnb market dynamics.


1. [Dashboard link](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/mounika.avadutha/viz/airbnb2_17377048912820/Dashboard2)
2. [Dataset Link](http://insideairbnb.com/get-the-data/)

## 1.Overview of Listings
### Total Listings:
Chicago has 6,593 listings.
New Orleans has only 245 listings a much smaller market.
### Room Types:
In both cities, most listings are Entire Home/Apt (about 84% in Chicago), which means full homes are mainly rented out.

## 2. Pricing Analysis
### Average Price per Night:

Chicago: Around $151.60 — relatively affordable for a big city.

New Orleans: Around $346.60 — much higher, possibly due to fewer listings or unique experiences.

## 3. Neighborhood Insights
Chicago's Top Neighborhoods:
Near North Side: 916 listings, $346.60 average price.
Loop: 515 listings, $295.50.
West Town: 772 listings, $278.00.
New Orleans' Top Neighborhood:
Central City: 629 listings, $197.10.
These differences show each city’s unique market and guest preferences.

## 4. Host Analysis
### Host Count:
Chicago has many hosts. One major brand, Blueground, has around 175 listings.

### Host Type Insights:
By looking at host types, prices, and number of reviews, we can understand which hosts are more popular or successful.

## 5. Review Patterns
### Reviews:
Listings in Chicago tend to have more reviews, showing higher guest activity.
New Orleans has fewer and more mixed review counts.

## 6. Visual Trends
### Charts & Graphs:
Heatmaps show where listings are most common and how much they cost.
Correlation plots (relationship charts) help see how things like price and reviews are connected to host types.

## Conclusion
## Market Style:
Chicago has a larger and more affordable Airbnb market.
New Orleans is smaller but more premium-priced, appealing to a specific type of traveler.

## Strategic Insights:
This data can help in deciding how to price, where to invest, or how to market listings better.

## Suggestions for the Future
### Track Changes:
Regularly update data on listings, prices, and reviews to stay ahead.
### Expand the Study:
Compare with other cities to get a better understanding of Airbnb markets.
